micro-flow — webhook inbox demo

A fictional payment provider, Paystream, sends signed webhooks to the server. The receiver checks the signature, drops duplicates and acks right away. Two worker workflows then drain the queue, and each event runs in its own workflow. Everyone shares one inbox.

Paystream console POST /api/webhooks/simulate

Send an event to see the receiver's response.

Send your own with curl

The receiver is a real endpoint. Sign <timestamp>.<body> with HMAC-SHA256 and the demo secret whsec_demo_microflow, and send it in the paystream-signature header as t=<timestamp>,v1=<hex>.
